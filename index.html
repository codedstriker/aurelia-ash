The Sanctuary Concept

Twelve Seats.
One Living Hearth. Zero Compromise.

We dismantled the barrier between the brigade and the diner. At Aurelia & Ash, dining is an intimate architectural rite. Guests sit along a monolithic counter sculpted from 300-year-old fallen Danish oak, inches away from coals harvested from Wakayama forests.

Every component is either transformed by immediate elemental flame or surrendered to years of slow microbial digestion inside our subterranean koji library.
