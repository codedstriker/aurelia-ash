Where It Began

The Copenhagen–Kyoto Dialogue

In the winter of 2017, Danish creative director Henrik Lindqvist traveled to the northern hills of Kyoto to study the preservation of mountain sansho pepper and centuries-old cedarwood smokehouses. It was there he encountered Chef Kenji Morimoto, an eighth-generation hearth cook who had spent three decades unlocking the thermal secrets of Kishu Binchotan oak charcoal.

What began as a late-night exchange over roasted wild mushrooms and cedar-aged sake became a radical manifesto: to fuse the austere foraged terroir of Scandinavia—the kelps, sea buckthorn berries, and wild lichens—with the disciplined, spiritual warmth of Japanese open-hearth cuisine.

Neither kitchen would dilute the other. Instead, fire would act as the catalyst, and fermentation would act as the memory.

Chrono-Architecture

The Evolution of the Craft

From an underground Copenhagen warehouse test kitchen to our permanent architectural estate in Kyoto.

2018

The Nordic-Kyoto Atelier

An 8-seat clandestine pop-up established in Copenhagen's Refshaleøen shipyard. Cooking solely over imported binchotan inside a raw concrete shell, testing forty varieties of wild Danish seaweed against house-grown koji strains.

2020

Shigaraki Fermentation Chambers

Commissioning master ceramic artisans in Shigaraki, Japan, to build bespoke unglazed earthenware jars. These micro-porous vessels harbor proprietary cultures of Aspergillus oryzae, incubating our inaugural batch of 24-month venison garums.

2022

Forager Alliances & Terroir Mapping

Formalizing our botanical network across Gotland, Bornholm, and Hokkaido. Securing exclusive sourcing rights for deep-sea kelp, sub-polar pine shoots, and uncultivated cloudberries from biodynamic preserves.

2024

The Kyoto Flagship Estate

The unveiling of our permanent twelve-seat hearth sanctuary in Kyoto's historical foothills. A custom masonry hearth engineered to burn aged white oak at 900°C alongside a subterranean microbial aging library.

Terroir & Ecosystem Stewardship

Honor the Soil. Revere the Tide.

True luxury is traceability. We do not purchase through commodity distributors; our larder is sustained exclusively by generational growers, coastal free-divers, and wilderness custodians.

Regenerative Earth
01

Biodynamic Heritage Farms

Partnered with zero-chemical estates in Bornholm and Nagano. Vegetables are harvested at sunrise and dispatched to our kitchen within eighteen hours, preserving native soil microbes.

Ethical Catch
02

Artisanal Ike-Jime Divers

All coastal bounty is dispatched via instantaneous neurological Ike-jime technique by 3rd-generation divers in Hokkaido and the Faroe Islands, guaranteeing pristine muscular tension and sweetness.

Forest Commons
03

Living Forest Foragers

Our team roams sub-alpine ridges and coastal dunes weekly, gathering wild juniper boughs, pine-bark cambium, rock moss, and elderberries at the peak of aromatic volatile oil concentration.
